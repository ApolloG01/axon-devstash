# UI Review — Axon DevStash (Live Playwright, 2026-05-12)

Screenshots saved to `docs/screenshots/`.

## Overall Verdict

The app is visually clean and well-designed for a developer audience. The dashboard and collection pages are solid. Three issues need immediate attention before launch: the homepage Features/AI sections are invisible (rendering bug), the mobile top bar overflows, and the sign-in form has excessive dead space and a visually inconsistent submit button.

## Findings

### Critical

**1. Homepage: Features and AISection are entirely invisible.**
Between the hero and the pricing section, there is a large block of empty black space — the Features grid and AI section simply don't render visually. This is likely the `ScrollAnimation` IntersectionObserver not triggering (possibly because the observer fires before elements are in view, or the initial opacity:0 is never lifted). A first-time visitor sees: hero → black void → pricing. Affects all desktop and mobile viewports.

**2. Mobile top bar overflows at 375px.**
At 375px width, the hamburger, logo, compressed search bar, star icon, "+ New Collection", and "+ New Item" buttons all compete for a single row. The "+ New Item" button is clipped off the right edge and unreachable. The search bar is also squished to near-unusable width. The top bar needs a responsive strategy at mobile widths (hide labels, drop secondary actions into hamburger, etc.).

**3. Sign-in form has ~300px of dead space above it.**
The form renders in the bottom half of the viewport. A visitor sees a near-empty dark screen with a tiny navbar, then has to scan down to find the form. This is caused by the auth layout's top padding combined with flex vertical centering. The form should sit visually near the center or upper-center of the screen.

---

### Major

**4. Sign-in submit button is white in dark mode.**
The "Sign in" button renders with a white/light background (`bg-primary` resolves to a light color in this context). Every other button in the app uses the blue primary or a dark outline. The white button looks like it belongs in light mode and feels like a visual bug.

**5. Stats cards have no color — all four icons are the same muted gray.**
On the dashboard, all four stat cards (Total Items, Collections, Favorite Items, Fav Collections) use an identical gray icon inside a gray square. There is no visual differentiation between them at a glance. Adding accent colors (e.g. amber for favorites, blue for items) would make the section scannable in under a second.

**6. Sidebar section labels have inconsistent left indent.**
`TYPES` and `COLLECTIONS` section headers render at visibly different x-positions — `TYPES` sits slightly further left than `COLLECTIONS`. The two labels don't share the same alignment, which creates a subtle but noticeable visual inconsistency in the sidebar.

**7. Sign-in page has no visible heading.**
The only text anchoring the form is a small muted `Sign in to your account` subtitle — no bold `<h1>`. First-time visitors have no typographic anchor. The form floats without a clear title.

**8. Homepage hero has ~200px of dead vertical padding above the content.**
The hero content (badge, headline, CTAs, mockup) starts well below the fold on initial load. The space between the navbar and the badge reads as an empty zone before the page comes alive.

---

### Minor

**9. Tablet (768px) works well.**
At 768px the sidebar, top bar, and 2-column item grid all fit cleanly. No overflow or clipping observed. This breakpoint is well handled.

**10. Collections page "New Collection" button is right-aligned but tab-stops inconsistently.**
The "+ New Collection" button in the collections page header floats to the far right with no visual grouping with the page title. On the dashboard the same button is in the top bar — the two placements are inconsistent.

**11. Item cards show hover-only copy button.**
The copy icon on item cards is invisible until hover, confirmed by Playwright snapshot data. On touch devices this action is permanently hidden.

**12. Settings page billing section missing savings callout.**
The monthly/yearly toggle shows "Monthly — $8/mo" and "Yearly — $72/yr" as plain buttons with no "Save 25%" highlight. The `/upgrade` page has a more prominent pricing toggle with a savings badge. The inconsistency means users finding billing via Settings miss the incentive to go yearly.

**13. Footer text is very low contrast.**
The footer text, link columns (Product, Resources, Company), and copyright line all render in very dark gray on the near-black background, visible in the full-page homepage screenshot. Contrast is below 4.5:1 for most footer text.

## Layout Suggestions (Actionable)

1. **Fix the ScrollAnimation trigger** — add `threshold: 0` to the IntersectionObserver options, or set `rootMargin: '0px 0px -50px 0px'` so sections animate in before they're fully scrolled to. Alternatively add `prefers-reduced-motion` check and skip the animation entirely for affected users, which would also fix the blank-sections bug.

2. **Mobile top bar: hide "+ New Collection" and "+ New Item" labels, keep only icons** below `sm` breakpoint. Or collapse both into the hamburger menu. The star and Upgrade link should also be deprioritized on mobile.

3. **Auth layout: remove `pt-[60px]` or reduce it to `pt-4`** and rely purely on `items-center justify-center` for centering. This will pull the form closer to the viewport center.

4. **Fix sign-in button color** — it should use `variant="default"` with the blue primary, not the current white/light rendering. Check that `bg-primary` resolves correctly in the auth layout's theme context.

5. **Add a bold `<h1>` to sign-in and register** — e.g. `text-2xl font-semibold text-foreground` above the subtitle. This gives the form a visual anchor and fixes the typographic hierarchy.

6. **Color-code stats card icons**: blue for Total Items, green for Collections, amber for Favorite Items, amber for Fav Collections. No layout change needed — just swap `text-muted-foreground` for the appropriate accent color per card.

7. **Normalize sidebar section label indentation** — give `TYPES` and `COLLECTIONS` headings identical `px-3` parent containers so they align flush at the same x position.

8. **Add `@media (hover: none)` CSS** to force `opacity-100` on the copy button in `ItemCard` and the star/3-dot buttons in `CollectionCard` so touch users can access them.

9. **Footer contrast** — raise footer body text from ~`#555` to at least `#888` (`text-muted-foreground`) and make footer link columns use `text-sm text-foreground/70` for legibility.

10. **Make pricing toggle consistent** — either bring the Settings billing section's interval toggle in line with the `/upgrade` page (add "Save 25%" badge), or simply link "Manage Billing" from Settings to `/upgrade` so users see the full pricing context.
